Partners

Connections that open conversations.

Our work is made possible by institutions in Germany and Pakistan that share our belief in language, education and cultural exchange.

Cooperation partner

Goethe-Institut Pakistan

ASH is a cooperation partner of the Goethe-Institut, Germany's cultural institute. Our courses follow its curriculum and prepare learners for Goethe examinations.

goethe.de

Academic exchange

DAAD

The German Academic Exchange Service supports university partnerships, research cooperation and student scholarships.

daad.de

pakistan.diplo.de

School network

PASCH, Schulen: Partner der Zukunft

pasch-net.de
