About us

A house for language and dialogue.

Since 2003, Annemarie-Schimmel-Haus has been Lahore's German cultural centre, a place where people learn German, meet each other and discover new perspectives.

Our story

Founded in Lahore, connected with Germany.

Annemarie-Schimmel-Haus (ASH) was set up in 2003 by its director, Naurin Ahmad-Zaki, who is also a permanent member of the Pak-German Society Lahore.

As a cooperation partner of the Goethe-Institut, ASH teaches German from A1 to C1 and prepares learners for the Goethe-Zertifikat. We work closely with the German Embassy in Islamabad, the German Consulate in Karachi, the DAAD and our neighbours on Scotch Corner, the Alliance Française de Lahore.

The name

Annemarie Schimmel (1922 to 2003)

Annemarie Schimmel was a German scholar of Islamic culture, mysticism and poetry. Her translations and studies of Jalaluddin Rumi and Muhammad Iqbal opened these worlds to German readers, and her lifelong affection for Pakistan made her a bridge between our two countries. The Haus carries her name as a promise: curiosity, respect and conversation across cultures.

What we do

Language

German courses from A1 to C1, on campus and online, placement tests and preparation for Goethe-Institut exams.

Culture

Concerts, film evenings, readings, exhibitions and festivals that bring German and Pakistani artists together.

Exchange

Guidance on studying and working in Germany, Open Days with our partners and school networks such as PASCH.

Literature

Writing workshops and readings with visiting authors, at the Haus and with partner universities in Lahore.

Our campus

Classrooms with a story.

Our classrooms carry the names of personalities who shaped European culture, from Johannes Gutenberg and Beethoven to Marlene Dietrich and Fatih Akin. Between them: a garden for breaks, conversation practice and summer events.
