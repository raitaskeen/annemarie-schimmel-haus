How it works

Registration in four steps

01

Choose your batch

Pick a level, a format (on campus or online) and a start date that suits you.

02

Apply online

Fill in the form and upload your CNIC, photo and last degree, about seven minutes.

03

Pay to confirm

You receive payment details by email. Pay within 24 hours to confirm your seat.

04

Start learning

Join your first class. Certificates are issued digitally on completion.

Information we ask for

  • Full name and father's / husband's name
  • CNIC or B-Form number
  • Date and place of birth
  • WhatsApp number and email
  • Emergency contact
  • Education and residential address
  • A short statement: why do you want to learn German?
