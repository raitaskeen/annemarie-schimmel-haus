Our story

Named after a bridge-builder.

Annemarie Schimmel (1922 to 2003) devoted her life to the poetry and mysticism of the Islamic world, from Rumi to Muhammad Iqbal, and remains deeply loved in Pakistan. In her spirit, the Haus brings Germans and Pakistanis together through language, literature, music and everyday encounters.

Founded in 2003 by its director Naurin Ahmad-Zaki, ASH works closely with the Goethe-Institut, the German Embassy, DAAD and our neighbours at Alliance Française de Lahore.

Our contribution

A bridge between Pakistan and Germany.

For more than two decades, Annemarie-Schimmel-Haus has opened Germany to learners across Pakistan and brought Pakistan's voices to German partners, through language, literature, music and education.

01

German for the whole country

Learners from Lahore and from across Pakistan study with us on campus and online, from their first words at A1 to C1, taught to Goethe-Institut standards by teachers who keep training with us.

02

Culture on the big stages

German authors at the Lahore Literary Festival, concerts such as “Berlin to Lahore”, German cinema and exhibitions: the Haus puts German-Pakistani dialogue where the public can join it.

03

Pathways to Germany

Information sessions on studying and working in Germany, visits to schools and universities, and exam preparation help young Pakistanis plan their next step with confidence.

04

Trusted by German partners

We work hand in hand with the Goethe-Institut, the German Embassy and DAAD, and welcome German writers, musicians and experts to Lahore every year.
